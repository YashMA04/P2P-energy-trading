# What was tried and discarded on evidence (v2)

Every row below was measured in the simulator on the deterministic reporting
day (battery-neutral cost unless stated). Nothing was dropped on argument alone.
Commands to regenerate each number are given.

| Idea | Measured | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-MAT as originally proposed**: MAT justified by "agents quote blind in the auction" | Replacing every v1 MAPPO checkpoint's learned quotes with the rule's fixed quotes changes community cost by −€0.76 to +€0.03/day and *raises* market utilisation (0.86–0.99 → 0.98–1.00) | Rationale discarded; MAT kept for a different, measured reason | Physical dispatch is settled before the market clears; under uniform pricing the clearing price is a transfer, so community welfare depends on quotes only through the 1% fee (< €0.5/day). MAT's value here is representing the *joint, non-unique* optimal schedule (see report §3), not auction coordination. `scripts/phase0_diagnostics.py`, quote ablation in the Phase-0 log |
| **ALaM state-wise multiplier network** for the seed-4 unserved energy | Seed 4's 0.42 kWh occurred at 23:30 with 41 vehicles shielded at once | Not built | Root cause was three defects in the per-vehicle shield (full setpoint made firm; non-minimal requirement; final-step off-by-one) plus the absence of a fleet-level check. The v2 community shield gives zero unserved energy for *every* policy tested, including uniformly random actions and adversarial deferral (103.5 → 0 kWh). A priced constraint cannot give that guarantee. `tests/test_v2_shield.py` |
| **Safety critic** (MA-DELC style) | v1 A/B: €149.47 vs €148.37 | Stays discarded | Re-proposed without a new reason; v1 evidence stands |
| **Plain MAPPO-v2** (v1 + shield + KL guard + episodic IR + validation selection) | Best €145.57 / €148.56, final €151.17 / €150.64 (2 seeds); fixed rule controller €143.90 | Kept as a baseline row, not as the method | The collapse is gone (final within €2–6 of best, vs €50+ in v1), but model-free PPO from the rule controller never beats the fixed rule controller. `scripts/train_v2.py` |
| **Behaviour cloning alone** (MAPPO student, 48 or 96 teacher days) | €210.70 / €194.30 vs teacher €99.72; validation MSE 0.012–0.015 | Discarded as a method; kept as DAgger iteration 0 | Low imitation loss, unusable closed loop: the student trickle-charges every EV all day and exports 234 kWh at €0.045 that it bought at ~€0.20. Covariate shift plus a non-unique optimum. |
| **SoC-target action parameterisation** | €363.40 (teacher's own targets replay to within €0.13) | Discarded (kept behind `action_mode` flag) | Removes error accumulation but converts a 3% SoC-target error on a 40 kWh EV into ±5 kW charge/discharge chatter every step. |
| **Time-limited MILP incumbents as DAgger labels** | At one state the 5 s incumbent's cost-to-go was €134.52; the proven optimum was €7.24 | Discarded; labels require a proven gap ≤ 0.1% | A label is only as good as the plan behind it. 7–64 relabels per 192 were rejected per iteration. `tests/test_v2_optimisation.py::test_time_limited_incumbent_is_not_marked_proven` |
| **MPC with proven-only plans** (convex fallback otherwise) | €143.07 vs €135.93 when the incumbent is used | The stronger variant is the reported comparator | The convex fallback plans V2G below the trip floor, which the simulator refuses. For *control* the feasible incumbent is better; we report the strongest MPC so the comparison is not flattering to RL. `scripts/run_mpc.py [--proven-only]` |
| **Standard PPO fine-tuning** of the DAgger student | Start €117.91 → selected €119.25 → final €133.46 (transient €204 at update 30) | Discarded | Destroys a near-optimal policy at this budget. |
| **Anchored PPO fine-tuning** (lr 1e-5, σ = e^-3, permanent anchor) | Final €117.85 vs start €117.91; validation €118.68 vs €118.47; raw-bill IR 0.96 → 1.00 | Reported as neutral on cost | Holds the policy, does not improve it. Its measurable effect is raw-bill individual rationality, which the settlement layer already guarantees. |
| **Legacy LP bound** (mean tariff, no degradation, free starting inventory, unrestricted V2G and grid-to-peer resale) | €87.52 vs exact simulator-consistent €98.74 (simulator settles the exact plan at €99.72) | Replaced | The legacy bound let a cheap-tariff household import and resell 562 kWh/day to peers and discharge EVs below their trip requirement, neither of which the simulator allows. |
