# v2 results

All numbers below are regenerated by

```bash
python scripts/final_eval_v2.py     # table   -> results/v2/final_table.{txt,json}
python scripts/make_figures_v2.py   # figures -> results/v2/figures/
```

from the saved checkpoints (minutes, no training). The full training protocol
that produced those checkpoints is `bash scripts/run_v2_protocol.sh`
(resumable; about 12 h wall-clock on 2 CPU cores, most of it exact MILP relabelling).

**Protocol.** One deterministic reporting day (never used for training or
checkpoint selection). Checkpoints are selected on 8 held-out noisy validation
days; final checkpoints are reported alongside. Costs are battery-neutral (any
end-of-day battery drawdown valued at the cheapest tariff of the day). "Recovered"
is the share of the achievable saving between grid-only (€175.89) and the exact,
simulator-consistent, battery-neutral optimum (€98.74; the simulator settles
that plan at €99.72). Three seeds per learned method; 95% bootstrap CI over seeds.

## RL fine-tuning of the MAT student (the RL stage)

`bash scripts/run_ft_mat.sh` — anchored PPO (60 updates, critic warm-up 20,
actor lr 1e-5, KL anchor to the frozen DAgger policy). The fine-tuned model
replaces the DAgger model **only if it is cheaper on the validation days**
(rule in `src/control/models.py`, tested in `tests/test_v2_platform.py`).

| Seed | DAgger (val / test) | Best fine-tuned (val / test) | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 115.65 / 116.20 | 114.97 / 114.33 (update 60) | fine-tuned |
| 1 | 113.11 / 113.46 | 113.75 / 113.36 (update 20) | **DAgger** (fine-tuning rejected on validation) |
| 2 | 113.10 / 111.85 | 109.74 / 108.21 (update 50) | fine-tuned |

Mean over seeds €113.84 → **€112.00** (80.4% → 82.8% recovered), zero unserved
energy and shortfall. RL helps on 2 of 3 seeds; on seed 1 it did not improve the
validation cost, so the rule kept the pre-RL model — even though the last
fine-tuning update happens to be cheaper on the reporting day (€111.23). We report
the rule's choice, not the better-looking number. Unseen noisy days: €113.67 → €112.42.
The gain is real but modest (€1.84/day); the oracle-guided DAgger stage does most
of the work.

## Imitation-stage ablation (behaviour cloning only, before DAgger)

| Student | seed 0 | seed 1 | seed 2 | mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MAPPO (independent per-household policy) | 210.70 | 203.72 | 157.54 | 190.65 |
| MAT, least-laxity decoding order | 122.95 | 132.35 | 120.78 | 125.36 |
| MAT, fixed random decoding order | 122.08 | 124.71 | 117.88 | **121.56** |

* **Autoregressive joint policy vs independent policy: decisive** — every MAT
  seed beats every MAPPO seed (P = 1.00), by €65/day on average.
* **Decoding order: hypothesis falsified.** Least-laxity order does not beat a
  random order (P = 0.33). No ordering contribution is claimed.

## What each layer contributed (measured)

| Layer | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Community feasibility shield | Unserved energy 0.00 kWh for **every** policy, including uniformly random actions and adversarial deferral (103.5 → 0 kWh, fig. 3). On its own it moves the rule controller from 29.2% to 41.5%. |
| Exact teacher + DAgger | Behaviour cloning alone, MAPPO: −19% mean (seeds −45%, −36%, +24%). After DAgger: 77.6% (MAPPO), 80.4% (MAT). Fig. 2. |
| MAT student | −€65/day at the imitation stage; −€2.22/day after DAgger. |
| PPO fine-tuning | Neutral (anchored: €117.85 vs €117.91 start) or harmful (standard: €133.46 final). |
| Core settlement | Individual rationality 1.00 for every policy whenever the community saves. Bid-manipulation gains: rule controller €0.59 → €0.001/day; MAT student €0.81 → €0.12/day, and the residual €0.12 only occurs with deviations that *lower* community cost. Fig. 4. |

## Strategic robustness

| | Rule controller | MAT student |
|---|---:|---:|
| Best single-household gain, auction bill | €0.59/day | €0.81/day |
| Best single-household gain, core settlement | €0.001/day | €0.12/day (welfare-improving only) |
| Best gain from misreporting own load/EV data to the settlement | €0.65/day | €1.13/day |

The settlement removes the incentive to manipulate the market but moves the
attack surface to data reporting. The required mitigation — computing the
settlement from metered, not declared, data — is stated as a deployment condition.

## Limitations (repeated where they matter)

* **Single day.** The teacher, the student and the evaluation all use one day
  of data with 2% load / 4% PV perturbations. Nothing here demonstrates
  generalisation to other days, seasons or weather.
* **The oracle-guided student needs a teacher at training time** (a MILP with
  perfect foresight on training days) but acts causally at execution.
* **MAT is centrally executed** (operator-run controller). The hybrid fallback
  (above) covers loss of communication; it does not cover a faulty or malicious
  operator.
* **RL fine-tuning helps on 2 of 3 seeds** and adds €1.84/day on average.
* **MPC has more information inside its window** than the agents; the stronger
  of two MPC variants is reported.
* **n = 3 seeds**; the anchored/standard PPO rows are single-seed and the
  MAPPO-v2 row two seeds.
