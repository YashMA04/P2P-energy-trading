Cost is battery-neutral: battery energy used up during the day is bought back at the day's cheapest tariff. The bar is the saving against every home buying from the grid on its own.

Savings are shared by each home's share of the optimal community plan (a core-stable allocation), so no home pays more than it would on the grid alone whenever the community saves.
