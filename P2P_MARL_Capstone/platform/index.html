Cost is battery-neutral: battery energy used up during the day is bought back at the day's cheapest tariff, so a controller cannot look cheap by emptying the batteries. The bar is the saving against every home buying from the grid on its own.

End-of-day bills

Savings are shared by each home's share of the optimal community plan (a core-stable allocation), so no home pays more than it would on the grid alone whenever the community saves.
